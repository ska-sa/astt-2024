# ASTT-095: Front-dish laser distance-meter enclosure and mount

**Status:** TODO — mechanical fixture
**Area:** Hardware | Mechanical
**Priority:** HIGH — for validation after reliable motion

## Goal and scope

Mount the user's laser-equipped distance meter rigidly in front of the dish for
independent three-point/triangle checks. Reuse ASTT-032 bracket assets if suitable;
its completed laser bracket does not mean this new meter enclosure is built.

- Confirm actual meter dimensions/weight, attachment, beam origin and access.
- Provide secure, removable, repeatable mounting; record meter-to-dish pointing
  and range-origin offsets rather than assuming the beam matches the dish axis.
- Check clearance, cable strain and any balance/load change through both axes'
  permitted travel before powered checks. Coordinate ASTT-038 printing with teammates.
- Follow the meter's laser-class/operating instructions for the ASTT-060 setup.

## Out of scope

Automatic pointing, changed limits, invented tolerances or accuracy claims from fit alone.

## Acceptance criteria and verification

- [ ] Reviewed design fits the actual meter and front-dish attachment.
- [ ] Fixture is rigid, repeatably aligned, accessible and has documented offsets.
- [ ] Approved clearance/load checks pass before independent measurements.
- [ ] Fixture supports the agreed ASTT-060 three-point procedure.

Likely files: agreed CAD/print assets, photographs and mounting notes. Inspect fit
unpowered first; physical checks require operator approval/supervision.
